Working notes · Network epistemology of proof

Proofs after AlephZero: epistemic phase transitions in AI-era mathematics

Extending Viteri & DeDeo (Cognition 2022) to the mathematics humans and machines are now writing together: new dependency-network data from four Lean megaprojects, three generations of AI provers, and the first quantitative pass at the correspondence problem.

Prepared July 31, 2026 · analysis code & extracted graphs in session scratchpad · all networks re-derived from public repositories

Headline findings

  1. The α ≈ 2 re-use signature survives the AI transition — wherever proofs are constructed. Human-led Lean projects of 2023–26 (PFR α = 2.26, FLT 2.33, Sphere Packing 1.98, Equational-Theories human stratum 2.08) and even Gauss's agentic autoformalization (1.94; pure-AI Dim24 subtree 2.10) all reproduce the out-degree power law found from Euclid to Coq.
  2. It dies wherever proofs are found by search. The Equational Theories machine stratum (13,708 declarations from Vampire, brute force, rewriting) shatters into 11,568 disconnected fragments with zero internal edges in its four largest generation methods — certificate dust with no re-use, no hubs, no power law (α fit collapses to 1.44), and modularity 0.38.
  3. Dependency between strata is one-way. 31,776 edges flow human → machine (certificates consume the human framework); 619 flow back. In network terms the machine stratum is an epistemic downstream that nothing upstream relies upon.
  4. Blueprints carry the "Wiles signature." Within the same projects, the informal blueprint networks show α ≈ 3.3–4.6 — the same hub-deficit the 2022 paper found in Wiles's FLT text — while their Lean twins sit at α ≈ 2. Communication-grain and verification-grain mathematics have measurably different topology.
  5. AI provers converged on the human epistemic architecture in about two years. AlphaProof 2024: monolithic proofs, zero named intermediate claims, 2× human case-split rate. AlphaProof Nexus 2026: 97% of claims named, 3× higher naming density than expert human Lean, human-level case-splitting, claim re-use up to 17.
  6. Tracking fidelity — the correspondence problem, measured — degrades with machine-generated mass: informal blueprint edges realized as formal dependency paths fall from 74% (PFR) to 51% (Sphere) to 47% (FLT, in progress) to 37% (Equational Theories).

1Where this starts

The 2022 paper made three claims about how belief in a proof forms: proof networks have the topology (heavy-tailed re-use, α ≈ 2, high modularity) needed for an epistemic phase transition — a jump to near-certainty at realistic per-step error rates; modules act as firewalls that contain doubt; and deduction and abduction must stay balanced, or certainty collapses (the abductive paradox). Two later papers set the AI-era agenda: AlephZero and mathematical experience (DeDeo 2024) predicts automation transforms impasse, error, and aboutness; A correspondence problem for mathematical proof (DeDeo & Duede 2026) argues a formalization justifies a particular proof only if it represents its theorem and tracks its steps — and that AI-era pipelines weaken exactly the causal and explanatory provenance that tracking requires.

Between 2022 and 2026 the object of study changed under the instrument: mathlib passed two million lines; Tao's PFR formalization took three weeks; the Equational Theories Project (ETP) settled all 22,028,942 implications among 4,694 magma laws with humans, ATPs, and LLMs in one repository; AlphaProof reached IMO silver; Gauss finished the sphere-packing formalization in five days and autoformalized the dimension-24 case from the original paper in two. Every one of these projects publishes its full dependency structure. The 2022 methodology can simply be pointed at them.

2New data, two grains

For each of four flagship projects — PFR (teorth/pfr), FLT (ImperialCollegeLondon/FLT), Sphere Packing (human-led repo and Math Inc's AI-completed fork), and Equational Theories (teorth/equational_theories) — two networks were extracted:

  • Blueprint graphs (informal grain): nodes are statements in the human-written LaTeX blueprint; edges are the authors' own \uses{} declarations. These are the direct descendants of the hand-coded Euclid/Wiles/Herstein networks of 2022 — with the difference that the community now writes the network itself as a working document.
  • Lean declaration graphs (formal grain): nodes are named declarations (theorem/lemma/def …); edges are textual references from one declaration's statement or proof to another. AlphaProof's raw IMO 2024 outputs, human formalizations of the same three problems (J. Myers, mathlib Archive), and AlphaProof Nexus's 2026 research-level corpus (2,494 theorems) were analyzed at the finer intra-proof grain: named-claim networks inside each proof.

Method caveat. Lean graphs here are textual (no compilation), so edges mediated by simp-sets or mathlib intermediaries are missed and suffix-matching admits some noise; absolute edge counts are lower bounds and cross-corpus comparisons are the meaningful quantity. Statistics are computed on largest weakly-connected components; α by discrete Clauset–Shalizi–Newman MLE; belief dynamics by the paper's asymmetric-Ising Glauber model (pprior = 0.75, 10 sweeps); ΔL₁ firewall statistic per the 2022 appendix at β = 1.

Kernel-grain validation (post-publication update). PFR was subsequently compiled and its ground-truth dependency graph extracted from the kernel environment (Expr.getUsedConstants over every project constant's type and proof term). Verdict: textual edges are 89.5% precise against kernel truth with 53% recall (the missing half is instance resolution, notation, and tactic-mediated uses); kernel α = 2.46 ± 0.23 and Q = 0.55 agree with the textual 2.27 ± 0.22 and 0.59 — the network-level claims are robust. Tracking fidelity rises at kernel grain: 86% of PFR blueprint edges are realized (vs 74% textual), confirming the reported levels as lower bounds. PFR's declarations make 88,790 distinct references into mathlib/core (≈68 per declaration) — a direct measure of the library dependence discussed in §7C.

Interactive: the object itself

Below is the actual network the statistics in this report are computed on — the largest connected component of PFR at kernel grain: 1,106 compiled declarations (nodes, sized by how often they are reused) and 5,344 kernel-verified dependency edges. Press play to run the 2022 paper's belief model live on it: each declaration is a spin that flips toward agreement with its dependencies and dependents, with per-step error rate ε. Drag ε and watch the epistemic phase transition — above the critical point belief stays patchy and mottled; below it, conviction crystallizes across the whole proof at once.

Three readings of the table. First, the 2022 universality is not a human quirk: it re-appears in every corpus built by incremental, context-bounded construction — including the 2026 AI autoformalizer. Second, it is not a machine inevitability either: saturation provers and brute-force enumeration, which find each proof independently by global search, produce a topology unlike anything in the 2022 dataset — not a longer chain, not a flatter tree, but dust plus a few enormous definitional stars. Third, the one machine method with real internal structure (MagmaEgg, greedy constructions compiled from a human-designed metatheory, mean internal out-degree 4.4) shows the divide is not human-vs-machine but constructed-vs-found.

4Result: the phase transition survives; what it rests on shifts

Every connected network still supports an EPT, and every one still has firewalls (ΔL₁ ≈ +3 to +10, within the 2022 range). But in the machine stratum the basis of certainty has changed. A Vampire certificate is an island: no sibling paths, no re-enforcement, no abductive flow from things it enables — because it enables nothing. Its truth is pinned by exactly two things: the kernel check (in the model, an external field applied node-by-node, not a network effect) and the shared definitions it instantiates. All epistemic risk therefore concentrates in the definitional hubs — which is Commelin's "ceci n'est pas une pipe" worry (are the definitions about what we think they are?) made quantitative. A single mis-stated definition in ET touches up to 13,816 dependent results; nothing in the certificate layer could detect it, since certificates never cross-check each other. The firewall structure that lets human mathematics contain error is, in the certificate layer, replaced by a monoculture root.

The 2024 system solved the problems and wrote them the way search trees look: one monolithic proof per theorem, intermediate facts bound anonymously (each have: shadowing the last — literally un-addressable), double the human case-split rate, and — as its own annotators note in the mirror repo — "the agent wastes the next 16 lines proving then discarding a lemma." Where the human formalizer of the same theorems produced 57 small named lemmas, AlphaProof produced 3 sealed blocks. Per the EPT model, such a proof cannot host a phase transition for its reader: there is one path to every claim, so doubt decays nothing and certainty comes only from the kernel.

Two years later, Nexus writes mathematics whose skeleton is indistinguishable from — indeed more articulated than — expert human Lean: 97% of claims named (with human-readable names like exists_separablyGenerated_charZero), naming density 3× the human expert, case-splitting at human rates, and claims re-used up to 17 times. The obvious deflationary explanation — imitation of human training style — is incomplete: naming density overshoots human practice substantially. The more interesting hypothesis is that addressable, modular subgoals are instrumentally necessary for any bounded-context agentic prover — the machine rediscovered lemma culture for the same reason mathematicians invented it. If so, the 2022 topology is a signature of resource-rational proving as such, and human mathematics found it first because human working memory is the tightest bound.

6Result: the correspondence problem, measured

DeDeo & Duede's tracking condition — the formal proof should realize the informal proof's inferential steps — becomes measurable with blueprints: for each blueprint edge whose endpoints carry \lean{} tags, ask whether the formal graph contains a path between the tagged declarations.

PFR (91% of nodes tagged)74%
Sphere Packing (42% tagged)51%

Bars: share of testable informal dependency edges realized as formal paths (≤ 6 hops, project-internal). Textual extraction under-counts, so read the gradient, not the levels — kernel-grain validation on compiled PFR raises its level from 74% to 86% (direct edges 50% → 65%), leaving the gradient intact.

The gradient tracks machine involvement. PFR — the disciplined human speedrun where the blueprint was the coordination instrument — realizes three-quarters of its informal edges formally, at a resolution ratio of 4.6 formal declarations per informal statement. ET realizes barely a third, at a ratio of 80:1: the blueprint narrates the human-facing theory while the 22M-edge machine payload lives entirely outside the narrative. The same holds for representational churn: only 213 of the human repo's 816 declaration names survive in Math Inc's AI-completed sphere-packing repo. Formal mathematics is becoming a medium in which the informal account is one projection of a much larger object it only partially tracks — the Standard View's "corresponding derivation" increasingly corresponds to nothing a human wrote.

7Common themes

A. Boundedness, not humanity, makes the topology

The strongest cross-cutting regularity in the new data: every corpus produced by incremental construction under bounded context — Greek deduction, Coq libraries, human Lean, agentic AI — lands on α ≈ 2 with strong modularity; every corpus produced by global search or enumeration lands off it, in dust or stars. This reframes the 2022 universality as resource-rationality, converges with the "tinkering/reuse" generative model there, and explains the AI style transition of §5 without invoking taste: naming and modularity are what proving under a context window forces on any agent. It also predicts where AlephZero-style systems will and won't produce human-legible mathematics: legibility tracks the generator's boundedness, not its carbon.

B. Certainty is decoupling from understanding, node by node

The kernel check acts as a per-node external field: it can pin any node to "true" regardless of network support. The machine stratum shows what mathematics looks like when only the field remains — verified, believed, and structurally incapable of being understood through path-multiplicity, because the paths don't exist. Duede & Davey's warrant-without-surveyability, Avigad's understanding-as-abilities, and Tao's "proof by intimidation" worry are all statements about this decoupling; the network model gives it an order parameter (field-pinned belief vs. coherence-supported belief) and the ET data gives it an existence proof at 22-million-edge scale.

C. Aboutness risk concentrates as structure evaporates

When certificates share nothing but definitions, definitions inherit all systemic risk (§4) — the network-theoretic form of AlephZero's aboutness problem and of the LTE test-suite practice. Prediction: the load-bearing artifact of AI-era mathematics is not the proof but the definition-plus-test-suite, and "stress-testing definitions" will professionalize the way refereeing once did (the Sphere-Packing team's language is already there).

D. The blueprint is the new site of mathematical cognition

Blueprints are simultaneously: the community's shared mental model (Wiles-signature topology, written for communication), the coordination contract for strangers and machines (Habgood-Coote & Tanswell's group knowledge, executable), and the empirical interface where correspondence is negotiated (§6). They are hand-authored EPT networks with per-node formalization state and git timestamps — the richest belief-formation dataset the field has ever produced, and nobody has analyzed them dynamically yet.

8New hypotheses

H1 · Bounded-generator universality

α ≈ 2 + Q ≳ 0.6 emerges in any proof corpus whose generator has bounded working context and pays a cost for re-derivation; global-search generators produce α < 1.7 (stars) or no fit (dust).

Ablate context length in an open prover (Goedel-Prover-V2, DeepSeek-Prover) on a fixed theorem set; measure α, naming density, and modularity of the output corpus as functions of window size.

H2 · Two-grain law

For the same mathematics, αcommunication > αverification; the gap Δα measures how much shared background the community's discourse presupposes (Euclid's text, unusually explicit, had Δα ≈ 0; modern blueprints have Δα ≈ 1–2.7).

Compute Δα across the ~40 leanblueprint projects; correlate with community size, field maturity, and formalization speed. Prediction: Δα shrinks as autoformalization becomes the medium of record, because the formal object starts being the communication.

H3 · Definitional concentration

As machine-generated proof mass grows, epistemic load (betweenness, out-degree share) concentrates on definitional nodes; ecosystem failure modes shift from wrong-step to wrong-aboutness.

Longitudinal Gini of out-degree over mathlib/ETP git history; count historical incidents (quadratic-reciprocity-with-no-primes style) per era; measure test-suite-to-definition ratios as the adaptive response.

H4 · Zombie stratum

Field-pinned (verified, structureless) results accumulate as a stratum that is believed but never re-used — operationalizing "zombie mathematics." Re-use, not verification, predicts downstream value.

Already visible: 619 of 45,313 ET edges flow machine→human. Track five years of citation/ re-use flow into AI-proved results (Nexus's Erdős corpus is the natural cohort) vs. matched human results.

H5 · Instrumental modularity

Agentic provers name and modularize because addressable subgoals are required for search under bounded context — not (only) because training data looks that way. Naming density should therefore track the agent's decomposition depth, not its training corpus style.

Compare naming density across systems with similar training data but different architectures (whole-proof RL vs. subgoal-decomposition vs. agentic-loop); the 2024→2026 overshoot (0 → 17.7 per 100 lines, 3× human) is initial evidence.

H6 · Abductive flooding

Fluent AI arguments raise abductive confidence without adding deductive paths. The 2022 model predicts over-weighted abduction lowers attainable certainty — so communities exposed to plausible unverified AI proofs should rationally increase demand for formal checking, not decrease it.

The Leiden Declaration and the verification-first norms of 2025–26 are the predicted response. Measurable: referee checking budgets and formalization-before-announcement rates, pre/post 2024.

H7 · Impasse redistribution

Copilots eliminate local impasses; definitional adequacy and value selection remain. Time-at-impasse distributions shift from heavy-tailed to bimodal (instant or intractable), hollowing out the middle difficulty band where, per AlephZero, judgements of value are trained.

Lean interaction telemetry (time-between-tactics, sorry lifetimes in git) across cohorts with/without AI assistance; blueprint git histories give sorry-lifetime distributions back to 2021.

H8 · Firewalls engineered vs. evolved

Blueprint-engineered modularity yields the same ΔL₁ containment as evolved modularity (confirmed here: +3 to +10 across both), but relocates trust to interfaces: type signatures become the contract, and interface errors — not interior errors — become the dominant failure class.

Classify historical formalization bugs (mathlib issues, LTE/Sphere errata, Gauss's two caught source errors) as interior vs. interface/definitional; the model predicts a rising interface share.

9Proposed next experiments

  • Dynamic EPT on blueprint git histories. Every \leanok flip is a timestamped belief-state change on a known network. Fit the Ising dynamics to the actual percolation of formalization across PFR/FLT/Sphere/Carleson; test whether progress cascades from high-out-degree nodes as the model predicts. This is the first chance to test the 2022 dynamics, not just its statics.
  • Compiled-grain replication. Re-extract all networks with LeanDojo/ntp-toolkit (kernel-level premises) to close the textual-extraction gap; the mathlib-wide studies (arXiv:2604.24797; proofgraph.org, 323k declarations) provide the null model.
  • Comprehension experiment. Give mathematicians matched AlphaProof-2024-style (sealed) and Nexus-2026-style (articulated) proofs of the same statements; measure error-detection, recall, and transfer. Does machine-adopted modularity actually restore understanding, or only its appearance? ProofOptimizer's 87%-compression pairs supply controlled stimuli.
  • The Vampire-humanization corpus. Tao's challenge to humanize a 62-step Vampire proof (minimized to 20 steps, arXiv:2605.21200) is a paired found/constructed proof of one theorem — the ideal microscope for what "understanding-through-reuse" adds.
  • Cross-layer Ising model. Extend the 2022 model with a per-node kernel field h and a definitional layer shared across proofs; derive the phase diagram in (h, βdep, βimp) — where does coherence-based belief become irrelevant, and what happens to global certainty when a definitional hub flips?

10Limitations

  • Textual Lean extraction misses tactic-mediated and mathlib-routed edges; kernel-grain validation on compiled PFR confirms α and Q are robust (2.46 vs 2.27; 0.55 vs 0.59), textual edges are 89.5% precise with 53% recall, and tracking-fidelity levels are lower bounds (86% vs 74% for PFR). The other three projects remain textual-grain pending builds.
  • ET's machine stratum proves ~22M mostly-independent implications; some structurelessness is the task's, not the method's. The Dim24/Nexus corpora control for this: same task class as human work, structure retained.
  • Blueprint \uses edges are author-curated and sparser than actual dependence; the Wiles-like α may partly reflect curation. (So did Wiles's own citations — that is the point.)
  • Simulation settings replicate the 2022 choices (symmetric β, prior 0.75) without sensitivity sweeps over the asymmetric regime; the abductive-paradox contours were not re-derived here.
